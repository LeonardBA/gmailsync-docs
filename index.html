Privacy Policy

GmailSync is a personal, self-hosted utility designed exclusively for personal email synchronization and archiving.

Data Access: This application accesses your Gmail data solely to fetch and store emails locally on your private infrastructure.

Data Sharing: No personal data, emails, or credentials are shared, transmitted, or sold to any third parties.

Data Storage: All data retrieved remains strictly under your direct control on your local storage.

If you have any questions, you can contact the developer via GitHub.
